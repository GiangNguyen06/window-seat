        What could you see from your window?

        Drop in a flight from Flightradar24. Window Seat works out the plane's tilt in turns, the earth's curve, haze and the sun, then shows what you could see from each side: cities, mountains, lakes, sunrise.

        How do I get the file?
        1. Log in on flightradar24.com (downloads need a paid plan).
        2. Search your flight number, e.g. KL1605, and open its history page.
        3. Next to the flight you took, click KML (best: includes airline, route and aircraft) or CSV.
        4. Drop that file above.
